# Paid tools of an indie documentary/event filmmaker (Sony α7S II) and their 2026 prices

Note: Most vendors publish USD prices. EU prices usually run about the same number in EUR, VAT included (e.g. Premiere Pro is about €21.84 vs US$22.99). Research date: Nov 2026.

## Camera control / monitoring / focus-exposure apps

### Takeaway
For the α7S II, the camera-control spend is close to zero. Sony's paid Monitor & Control app (from $4.99/mo) targets newer bodies. The α7S II only works with the free (and limited) Imaging Edge Mobile/PlayMemories remote. So a companion app here mainly adds missing function rather than saving money. Most of the money goes on monitors and their hardware, and free phone-side tools such as the Blackmagic Camera app and the free Dehancer False Color plugin already exist.

### Cited Findings
- Sony Monitor & Control (Creators' Cloud) has a "Basic" plan at $4.99/month or $49.99/year. The price is set by the reseller, and the listed cameras are newer models such as the α7C II. — [Sony CCMC](https://www.sony.net/ccmc/)
- The FX3, FX30, α1, α7R V, α7S III and α7 IV moved from Imaging Edge Mobile to Creators' App. Older bodies stay on Imaging Edge Mobile. — [Sony Imaging Edge Mobile](https://imagingedge.sony.net/l/ie-mobile.html)
- Users report that Sony's remote apps crash and that the remote shutter is unreliable. They ask for alternatives, and third-party apps such as "Monitor+" say Wi-Fi is unsupported for some bodies. — [Reddit r/SonyAlpha](https://www.reddit.com/r/SonyAlpha/comments/1cgrs5q/is_it_worth_getting_the_sony_app_at_all_ios/); [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/SonyAlpha/comments/125xcua/any_apps_compatible_with_sony_alphas_ther_than/)
- Dehancer offers a free OFX tool for exposure monitoring with false color and clipping indication. — [Dehancer shop](https://www.dehancer.com/shop)
- The Blackmagic Camera app is free. — [Instagram roundup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C5ko1laI2VN/) (weak source)

### Inferences
- Realistic savings in this category: €0–60/year. A companion app is worth building for function (focus/exposure aids, remote control over the old Sony Camera Remote API), not as a cost replacement.

### Gaps
- I did not confirm from an official Sony list that Monitor & Control excludes the α7S II. This is inferred from the camera generation.
- I found no prices for Atomos, Accsoon or other monitoring software, or for paid iOS focus/exposure tools such as Cadrage.

## LUTs and color grading

### Takeaway
DaVinci Resolve (free) covers almost all grading needs. Resolve Studio is a one-time $295 purchase, $236 on sale until 20 Nov 2026. Film-emulation subscriptions such as Dehancer are the main recurring color cost. The only figure I found is the Studio plan at $49/month, or $588/year billed yearly.

### Cited Findings
- DaVinci Resolve Studio costs $295 one-time and is discounted to $236 through 20 Nov 2026. The free version handles most 8-bit workflows. — [Blackmagic](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/event/davinci-resolve-special); [The Post Flow](https://thepostflow.com/post-production/video-editing/resolve-studio-vs-free/); [YMCinema](https://ymcinema.com/2026/10/21/blackmagic-davinci-resolve-studio-ai-editor/)
- Adobe Premiere single app costs US$22.99/month on an annual plan billed monthly, about €21.84 in the EU. Creative Cloud Pro costs about $69.99/month. — [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/plans.html); [Photutorial](https://photutorial.com/premiere-pro-pricing-explained/)
- Dehancer Studio subscription: $49/month billed $588/year, covering all video and photo plugins on one seat. — [Dehancer pricing](https://www.dehancer.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- A switch from Premiere to Resolve free saves about €260/year. Replacing Dehancer with free LUTs and a CST workflow saves up to $588/year, if that tool was in use.
- A LUT-management feature in the companion app (import, preview S-Log2/S-Gamut LUTs, export .cube) replaces paid LUT packs. Those are typically one-off purchases, but I did not price them.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable 2026 prices for commercial LUT packs or LUT-management apps.

## Shot logging, slate/timecode, call sheets and shot lists

### Takeaway
MovieSlate 8 Pro costs a lot: $24.99/month or $299.99 one-time. StudioBinder's paid tiers are aimed at teams; the free plan caps shot lists at 10 shots. Shot Designer Pro is cheap at €19.99 one-time. A companion app with shot logging, a slate and a shot list could replace most of this.

### Cited Findings
- MovieSlate 8 subscription: $24.99/month, or $299.99 one-time. Its own specification page also lists other figures ($9.99/mo, $26.99/mo), so the tiers are unclear. — [Pro Filmmaker Apps](https://profilmmakerapps.com/app/movieslate/); [movie-slate.com](http://www.movie-slate.com/Specifications); [MovieSlate Pro features](https://www.movie-slate.com/MovieSlate_PRO_Features)
- Free slate apps exist, such as "No Budget Slate". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/no-budget-slate/id590309536)
- StudioBinder's free plan limits shot lists to 10 shots. Paid tiers listed by TrustRadius: Agency $249/mo, Production Company $399/mo, Enterprise $1,499/mo. These figures may be outdated; the official page showed a garbled "Studio" price for 20 users and 400 GB. — [StudioBinder support](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/10106468-choosing-the-right-studiobinder-plan); [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/studiobinder/pricing); [StudioBinder pricing](https://app.studiobinder.com/pricing)
- Shot Designer has a free version, and the Pro version costs €19.99. — [Hollywood Camera Work](https://www.hollywoodcamerawork.com/shot-designer.html)

### Inferences
- A solo filmmaker who would otherwise use MovieSlate Pro on subscription could save about $300/year. StudioBinder's paid tiers are unrealistic for solo work, so most solo filmmakers use the free tier or spreadsheets.

### Gaps
- I could not verify StudioBinder's current entry-level (Indie/Pro) price for 2026. The official page did not render cleanly.

## Review/delivery, cloud storage, transcription, music

### Takeaway
These are the largest recurring costs:
- Music: €120–480/year
- Frame.io Pro: $15 per seat per month
- Cloud storage: about $120/year
- Transcription: $100–350/year

Transcription is the most clearly replaceable item, through local Whisper. Music licensing is the hardest to replace.

### Cited Findings
- Frame.io: Free ($0, 2 members, 2 GB), Pro ($15 per member per month, about $13 billed annually, up to 5 members, 2 TB), Team ($25 per member per month). — [Frame.io](https://frame.io/pricing); [Brault](https://brault.app/blog/creative-asset-management/frame-io-pricing); [Krock](https://krock.io/frame-io-pricing/)
- Dropbox Plus: $9.99/month for 2 TB. — [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/plans); [Activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/dropbox-pricing-2024-every-plan-and-hidden-cost-compared)
- Sony Creators' Cloud gives 5 GB free (25 GB for eligible Sony users), with paid tiers of 100 GB and 500 GB. — [Alpha Universe](https://alphauniverse.com/stories/sony-announces-major-updates-to-monitor-and-control-creators-app/)
- HappyScribe subscriptions:

  | Plan | Monthly billing | Billed annually | AI minutes/month |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Basic | $17 | $8.50 | 120 |
  | Pro | $29 | $19 | 600 |

  Top-up minutes cost $0.20 each, and human transcription costs from $2/min. Rev charges about $0.25/min for AI and about $1.50/min for human transcription. — [HappyScribe](https://www.happyscribe.com/pricing); [Sonix](https://sonix.ai/resources/happy-scribe-review-2/); [Vocap](https://vocap.io/en/blog/ai-transcription-pricing-cost-comparison-guide)
- Epidemic Sound: Creator $9.99/mo yearly ($17.99 monthly); Pro $16.99/mo yearly ($39.99 monthly); Business $30/mo yearly ($75 monthly). Artlist: Music & SFX from $9.99/mo; Max about $39.99–50.66/mo yearly (sources differ). Musicbed does not publish prices. — [Epidemic Sound](https://www.epidemicsound.com/pricing/); [Epidemic blog](https://www.epidemicsound.com/blog/artlist-vs-epidemic-sound/); [The Post Flow](https://thepostflow.com/post-production/video-editing/music-subscriptions-compared/)

### Inferences
- Local Whisper transcription with SRT export could replace HappyScribe Basic or Pro, saving about $100–350/year.
- A self-hosted review link (e.g. Nextcloud) or Frame.io Free could replace Frame.io Pro, saving about $180/year per seat.
- Music needs a licence. Paid client work typically needs Epidemic Pro or Business, or Artlist, at about $200–360/year, which is hard to replace with free tools. CC0 and free libraries carry licence risk for commercial work.

### Gaps
- I did not verify EUR checkout prices for Frame.io or Dropbox.

## Strong free alternatives and a rough savings estimate

### Takeaway
Strong free substitutes exist for:
- NLE and grading: DaVinci Resolve free
- Transcription: Whisper
- Review: Frame.io Free or self-hosted
- Shot diagrams: Shot Designer free
- Slate: free slate apps
- Exposure tools: Dehancer False Color

Weak substitutes exist for licensed music and large-scale cloud storage.

### Cited Findings
- DaVinci Resolve free version — [Blackmagic](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)
- Frame.io Free tier (2 GB) — [Frame.io](https://frame.io/pricing)
- Shot Designer free version — [Hollywood Camera Work](https://www.hollywoodcamerawork.com/shot-designer.html)
- Dehancer free False Color tool — [Dehancer shop](https://www.dehancer.com/shop)

### Inferences
Plausible annual paid stack for a solo studio, using the prices above:

| Item | Annual cost | Plausibly replaceable? |
|---|---|---|
| Premiere | about $276 | yes |
| Frame.io Pro, 1 seat | $156–180 | yes |
| Dropbox Plus | $120 | partly |
| HappyScribe Pro (annual billing) | $228 | yes |
| Epidemic Pro | $204 | no |
| MovieSlate Pro | about $300 | yes |
| Sony Monitor & Control | $50, likely not applicable to the α7S II | n/a |
| Dehancer (optional) | $588 | partly |

- Gross total: about €1,300–1,900/year.
- Plausibly replaceable with a companion app plus a free toolchain: about €700–1,100/year (Premiere, MovieSlate, transcription, Frame.io, part of the storage cost).
- The companion app itself accounts for the slate/log/shot-list/LUT/monitoring part, roughly €300–350/year. The rest comes from switching to the free toolchain.

### Gaps
- I found no survey data on which of these tools solo filmmakers in Germany or Austria actually pay for. The stack above is an assumption.
